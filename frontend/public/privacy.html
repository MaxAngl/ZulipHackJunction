Needed · Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Needed is a prototype built during the Junction 2026 hackathon, for people living at home after depression treatment. It covers the Needed web app and its Garmin watch app. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, monitor or treat any condition.

What we collect

  • What you enter in the app: first name, interests, profile details and your answers to requests.
  • From the watch app, only once you link it to your account with a 6-digit code and only while the app is open: heart rate, stress level, Body Battery, breathing rate, steps, floors climbed, intensity minutes, resting heart rate and the local time of the reading.
  • A random token that identifies your device. There is no password and no location tracking.

Why

To estimate, from everyday signals, how much the app should ask of you, and to show the requests meant for you on your phone and your watch. Your measurements are never shown to the people who send you requests.

Where it goes

Data is stored on the Needed prototype server only. It is not sold or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

How long

This is a hackathon prototype: the data is test data and is deleted when the prototype is shut down.

Your choices

You can unlink your watch at any time from your profile (“Unlink watch”), or uninstall the watch app. The watch stops sending data immediately.
